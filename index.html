Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got clear, direct answers.

How is the one-way distance calculated?

We calculate distance using real-time spatial matrix technology between your precise pickup location and drop destination. You pay solely for that calculated kilometer distance.

Are Toll fees and State Permit Taxes included?

Toll fees and Interstate Permit Charges (for Andhra Pradesh/Karnataka entries) are collected as actuals based on government highway norms, paid directly at toll booths.

Is there any night driving charge or driver allowance?

A standard flat driver bata of ₹400 per day applies to outstation trips to cover driver expenses.
